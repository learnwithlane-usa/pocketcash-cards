Consignment & Order Details

Have Questions? We Have Answers!

Text 3 photos of your $20+ singles or graded slabs to (405) 459-7176. Once approved, we meet in person in Ardmore, Durant, Mead, Madill, Kingston, Sherman, or Denison, verify each card together, place them in our padded Zion slab case, and store them in a locked fire-rated safe while they sell on our eBay storefront.
You keep 85% of net proceeds on $500+ graded slabs (7-day auction), 75% of net proceeds on standard 7-day auctions ($15–$499), or 65% of net proceeds on 30-day Buy-It-Now listings ($75+ minimum). There is a $5/card upfront intake fee covering high-res scans, 1080p vertical video, safe custody, and 1 free relist.
Standard payouts go out fee-free via Zelle on the 1st Friday of every month after the eBay return hold window clears, accompanied by a line-by-line settlement sheet. An optional Fast Payout (5% fee, $5 min) is available 3 days after USPS delivery confirmation on sales up to $250.
Thin raw singles ≤ $20 ship via eBay Standard Envelope with $1 combined shipping. Orders up to $100 ship double-padded in a padded mailer inside a padded mailer, and $100+ cards or slabs ship in a padded mailer inside a sturdy box via tracked USPS.
