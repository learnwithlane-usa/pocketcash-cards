Consignment FAQs

1. What cards qualify for Pocket Cash consignment? ▼

We accept PSA, BGS, SGC, and CGC graded slabs plus raw singles (sports, Pokémon, and non-sports) worth $20.00+ per card. Focusing on $20+ singles instead of $1–$5 bulk commons lets us give every card full scans, 1080p video, and auction marketing. Text photos to (405) 459-7176 if you'd like a quick eligibility check.

2. How do the 85%, 75%, and 65% tiers & relists work? ▼

On 7-day auctions, you keep 85% of Net Proceeds on $500+ graded slabs (15% commission) and 75% on $15–$499.99 cards (25% commission), including 1 free 7-day relist if unsold (or $0 return / $3 per extra cycle). For 30-day Buy-It-Now ($75 min), you keep 65% (35% commission, 90 days safe custody included).

3. How are my cards protected in custody and shipping? ▼

Cards are transported in our padded Zion Slab Case and stored inside a locked, fire-rated gun safe. Safe custody liability is capped at the lowest of Card Ladder value for the logged grade/Raw NM, 30–90 day eBay sold comps, or $200/card ($1,000 aggregate cap per consignor). Sold cards over $100 ship automatically insured for full sale value via tracked USPS.
