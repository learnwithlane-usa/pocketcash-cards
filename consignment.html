Consignment FAQs

1. What cards qualify for Pocket Cash consignment? ▼

We accept PSA, BGS, SGC, and CGC graded slabs plus raw singles (sports, Pokémon, and non-sports) worth $35.00+ per card with $0 upfront. Focusing on pre-screened $35+ cards instead of $1–$5 bulk commons lets us give every card high-res scans, clean 1080p HD video, armor packaging at $0 fee, and national eBay auction marketing. Text photos to (405) 459-7176 for a quick eligibility check.

2. How do the 85%, 75%, and 65% tiers & relists work? ▼

There is $0 upfront fee and $0 packaging fee. On 7-day auctions ($25 min starting bid, no reserves), you keep 85% of Net Proceeds on $500+ graded slabs (15% commission) and 75% on cards up to $499.99 (25% commission), including 1 free 7-day relist if unsold (or $0 return / $3 per extra cycle). For 30-day Buy-It-Now ($75 min), you keep 65% (35% commission, 90 days safe custody included).

3. How are my cards protected in custody and shipping? ▼

Cards are transported in our padded Zion Slab Case and stored inside a locked, fire-rated gun safe. Safe custody liability is capped at the lowest of Card Ladder value for the logged grade/Raw NM, 30–90 day eBay sold comps, or $200/card ($1,000 aggregate cap per consignor). Sold cards over $100 ship automatically insured for full sale value via tracked USPS.

4. How do I consign PSA, BGS, or SGC graded cards in Oklahoma? ▼

Text photos of your graded slabs ($35+ value) to (405) 459-7176 for a quick pre-screen, then drop them off in person at our Ardmore shop (Booth #64) or at a scheduled meet-up in Durant, Mead, Madill, Kingston, Sherman, or Denison. No forms to fill out ahead of time—we scan and log everything at handoff with $0 upfront. Slabs selling for $500.00+ qualify automatically for our 85% High-End Graded tier, and slabs under $500 keep 75% of Net Proceeds.

Yes. For inherited binders, vintage pre-1975 sets, and estate collections across Carter, Bryan, Marshall, and Grayson counties, we evaluate your cards in person so your top singles and slabs can earn 75%–85% via national eBay consignment.
