Frequently Asked Consignment Questions

1. What cards qualify for Pocket Cash consignment?

We accept PSA, BGS, SGC, and CGC graded slabs as well as raw singles (Baseball, Football, Basketball, Hockey, Soccer, Wrestling, Racing, Pokémon, and non-sports) with a realistic market value of $20.00 or more per card. We do not consign bulk $1–$5 commons so we can dedicate full photography, 1080p video, and marketing attention to every card we list. (Have bulk or lower-end collections? Check out our Cash Buyout Page!)

2. Why is the commission 25% on 7-Day Auctions vs. 35% on 30-Day Buy-It-Now?

7-day auctions generate fast bidding action and turn your cards into cash quickly without tying up safe space. To reward consignors who price their cards to sell at auction, we charge our lowest 25% commission on Net Proceeds (and include 1 free 7-day relist). For consignors who prefer to hold out for a fixed Buy-It-Now price ($75.00 minimum), our 35% commission includes up to 90 days of fire-rated safe custody.

3. How does safe custody & shipping liability work?

While in our locked, fire-rated gun safe, our bailee liability is governed by objective market data—never inflated guesswork—capped at the lowest of: (a) Card Ladder value for the exact logged slab grade (or Card Ladder Raw Near-Mint value if logged Raw NM on Schedule A), (b) verifiable 30-to-90-day eBay sold comps if Raw LP-or-Worse or not tracked on Card Ladder, or (c) $200.00 per card ($1,000.00 aggregate cap per consignor). Once sold and shipped via tracked USPS Ground Advantage or Priority Mail (cards over $100 are automatically insured for the full sale price), transit loss or damage is covered by the carrier's insurance proceeds.
