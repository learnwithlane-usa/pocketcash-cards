❓ Collector Insights & FAQ

Set Lore, Oddities & Print Run Notes

Q: What is Major League Table Tennis (MLTT)?

MLTT was founded in 2023 as the first professional table tennis league in the United States, featuring 8 teams split into Eastern and Western conferences and a unique high-energy 'Golden Game' team showdown.

Q: What parallels exist in 2024 Leaf Premier MLTT?

Cards feature Base Silver Foil, Red Foil /99, Blue Foil /50, Green Foil /25, Gold Foil /10, and 1-of-1 Black Super Prism parallels, alongside certified sticker and on-card autographs.
