2023-24 Upper Deck PWHL Inaugural Showcase

The landmark inaugural card set celebrating the launch season of the Professional Women's Hockey League (PWHL). ❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Is this the first officially licensed card set for the PWHL?

Yes! The 2023-24 Upper Deck Inaugural Showcase is the historic first officially licensed box set for the Professional Women's Hockey League.

What is the Walter Cup, and are champions featured?

The Walter Cup is the championship trophy of the PWHL. Inaugural champions PWHL Minnesota (led by Taylor Heise and Kendall Coyne Schofield) are featured prominently.
