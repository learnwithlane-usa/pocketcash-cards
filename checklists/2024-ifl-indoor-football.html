❓ Collector Insights & FAQ

Set Lore, Oddities & Print Run Notes

Q: Where are official IFL cards sold?

Team sets are produced in partnership with regional printers like Choice SportsCards and sold directly at team merchandise stands during home games, with remaining stock fulfilled via online team stores.

Q: Do IFL cards have secondary market value?

Yes! Because complete team sets often cost just $10–$15 at the arena, parting out standout MVP quarterbacks like Drew Powell or NFL alumni singles routinely generates $8–$20 per card on eBay.
