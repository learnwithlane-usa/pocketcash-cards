2023-24 Upper Deck PWHL Professional Women's Hockey League
Checklist & Value Guide

The historic 2023-24 Upper Deck PWHL set celebrates the groundbreaking inaugural season of the Professional Women's Hockey League. With franchises in Boston, Minnesota, Montreal, New York, Ottawa, and Toronto, this release features foundational first-year cards and certified autographs for Olympic gold medalists and world champions.

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Why is the 2023-24 Upper Deck PWHL set historically significant?

It is the first officially licensed league-wide trading card set for the Professional Women's Hockey League, representing the historic inaugural season for North American women's professional hockey.

What are the key chase cards to look for?

Taylor Heise's first-overall pick rookie card, Marie-Philip Poulin autographs, and High Gloss parallels numbered to just 10 copies.
