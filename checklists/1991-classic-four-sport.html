1991 Classic Four Sport Draft
Checklist & Value Guide

Classic Four Sport made history in 1991 by uniting the top draft prospects from Baseball, Basketball, Football, and Hockey into a single groundbreaking 250-card release. Headlined by early college appearances of Shaquille O'Neal (LSU), Brett Favre (Southern Miss), Eric Lindros (Oshawa Generals), and Manny Ramirez (George Washington HS).

📅 Release: 1991 📦 36 Packs Per Box / 12 Cards Per Pack 📊 12 Cards Tracked

🔥 Key Set Highlights & Chases

    Shaquille O'Neal (LSU Tigers): Shaq's true first widely distributed trading card, showcasing the dominant center at LSU.

    Manny Ramirez (High School Prospect): Early New York City high school baseball prospect card for the 500-home run champion.

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Why is 1991 Classic Four Sport considered a milestone set?

Prior to 1991, multi-sport sets were virtually nonexistent in the modern card industry. Classic was the first to bundle elite NCAA athletes and Canadian junior hockey prospects into one product.

Which card from 1991 Classic Four Sport has the highest PSA 10 value?

The Shaquille O'Neal #1 card in PSA 10 Gem Mint condition commands the highest price, followed closely by the Brett Favre #26 and Eric Lindros #101.
