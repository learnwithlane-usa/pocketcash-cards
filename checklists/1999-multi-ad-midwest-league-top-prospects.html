1999 Multi-Ad Midwest League Top Prospects
Checklist & Value Guide

Issued in 1999 by Multi-Ad Sports, the Midwest League Top Prospects 35-card factory set captures the premier Single-A talent across the Midwest League before their Major League breakouts. Headline chases include early minor league cards of two-time MLB All-Star slugger Adam Dunn, Gold Glove finalist Brandon Inge, All-Star shortstop Jack Wilson, Corey Patterson, and 2000 Olympic Gold Medalist Sean Burroughs.

  • #10
    Adam Dunn (462 MLB HRs): Two-time MLB All-Star slugger who crushed 462 career home runs.
  • #16
    Brandon Inge (MLB All-Star): Longtime Detroit Tigers third baseman, catcher, and 2009 AL All-Star.
  • #34
    Jack Wilson (MLB All-Star SS): Pittsburgh Pirates All-Star shortstop and Silver Slugger winner.
  • #26
    Corey Patterson (#3 Overall Pick): Chicago Cubs 1st Round pick (#3 overall) and 12-year MLB outfielder.
