❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Are the jersey cards in Upper Deck CFL game-worn?

Yes, UD Game Jersey cards feature authentic swatches cut from game-worn CFL jerseys certified on card back.

Why do Canadian football cards hold value?

Print runs are substantially lower than NFL releases, creating localized scarcity for star quarterbacks and Grey Cup champions.
