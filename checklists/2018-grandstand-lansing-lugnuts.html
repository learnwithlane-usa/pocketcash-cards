2018 Grandstand Lansing Lugnuts
Checklist & Value Guide

Produced by Grandstand Cards for the 2018 Midwest League season, this 31-card Lansing Lugnuts team set showcases the Toronto Blue Jays' Single-A roster. Key prospects who went on to reach Major League Baseball include power hitter Ryan Noda, speedster Samad Taylor, infielder Kevin Smith, pitchers Zach Logue, Maximo Castillo, Dany Jimenez, and Yennsy Diaz, alongside Kacy Clemens (son of Roger Clemens).

🔥 Key Set Highlights & Chases

  • Noda
    Ryan Noda (MLB 1B/OF): On-base machine and power hitter who broke out with Oakland in MLB.
  • Taylor
    Samad Taylor (MLB INF/OF): Dynamic speed prospect who debuted in MLB with Kansas City and Seattle.
  • Smith
    Kevin Smith (MLB INF): Former top Blue Jays infield prospect who played for Toronto, Oakland, and the Yankees.

    Kacy Clemens (#2): First baseman and son of 7-time Cy Young Award winner Roger Clemens.

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Which MLB affiliate was the Lansing Lugnuts in 2018?

In 2018, the Lansing Lugnuts served as the Single-A Midwest League affiliate of the Toronto Blue Jays before becoming the Oakland Athletics' High-A affiliate in 2021.

How many cards are in the 2018 Grandstand Lansing Lugnuts team set?

The complete team set contains 31 cards covering players, field staff, and mascot Big Lug.
