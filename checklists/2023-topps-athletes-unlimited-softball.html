❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

How does Athletes Unlimited softball scoring work?

Athletes Unlimited uses an innovative leaderboard scoring system where players earn individual points for in-game plays and team wins, crowning an individual champion at the end of the season.

Why are Topps AU Softball cards sought after?

It represents the very first modern, high-grade Topps release dedicated exclusively to women's professional softball, making rookie cards for legends like Cat Osterman and Odicci Alexander permanent historical artifacts.
