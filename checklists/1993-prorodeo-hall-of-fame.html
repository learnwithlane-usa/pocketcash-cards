1993 ProRodeo Hall of Fame Limited Edition Series
Checklist & Value Guide

Issued in 1993 to benefit the ProRodeo Hall of Fame in Colorado Springs, this limited edition card set chronicles the immortal founders, record-setters, and legendary stock that shaped 20th-century American rodeo.

🔥 Key Set Highlights & Chases

  • #1
    Freckles Brown (Rode Tornado): 1962 World Champion Bull Rider who famously rode the unrideable bull Tornado at age 46.
  • #2
    Casey Tibbs (9x World Champion): The flamboyant cowboy icon who graced the cover of LIFE Magazine and popularized modern saddle bronc riding.
  • #3
    Jim Shoulders (16x World Champion): Rodeo's undisputed all-time championship giant with 16 world titles across three events.
  • #4
    Larry Mahan (6x All-Around Champion): Rock-and-roll cowboy who revolutionized rodeo marketing and won 6 All-Around crowns.

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Who produced the 1993 ProRodeo Hall of Fame card set?

The set was produced in conjunction with the PRCA ProRodeo Hall of Fame to preserve Western history and honor inducted legends.

What is the most valuable card in the set?

Cards of Jim Shoulders, Casey Tibbs, and Freckles Brown command the highest collector premiums, particularly in sharp gem mint condition.
