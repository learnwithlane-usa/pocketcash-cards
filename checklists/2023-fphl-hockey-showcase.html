❓ Collector Insights & FAQ

Set Lore, Oddities & Print Run Notes

Q: Why do FPHL hockey cards command strong eBay premiums?

Team sets are printed in micro-runs (500 to 1,500 total sets) and sold exclusively at arena merchandise booths. Rabid hockey fight collectors and goaltender specialists actively seek individual player cards on the secondary market.

Q: How can I get these cards autographed?

Because FPHL arenas offer intimate access, fans frequently get these cards signed after games by waiting near the player tunnel or at official post-game booster club skate events.
