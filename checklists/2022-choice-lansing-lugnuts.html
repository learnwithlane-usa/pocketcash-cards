2022 Choice Lansing Lugnuts
Checklist & Value Guide

Issued by Choice SportsCards during the 2022 Midwest League season, this 36-card Lansing Lugnuts team set is loaded with future Oakland Athletics Major League core talent. Headline chases include breakout MLB slugger Lawrence Butler (#07), 1st-round catcher/first baseman Tyler Soderstrom (#26), MLB starting pitcher Joey Estes (#09), MLB pitchers Hogan Harris (#13) and Grant Holman (#15), plus an official on-card autograph of infielder Drew Swift (#27).
