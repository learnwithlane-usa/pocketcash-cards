About the 2026 Choice Banana Ball Set

Banana Ball is taking the baseball world by storm, selling out Major League Baseball stadiums nationwide. Produced by Choice SportsCards under official license from Fans First Entertainment, the 2026 Choice Banana Ball card release captures the energy, trick plays, and beloved stars of the Banana Ball Championship League (BBCL).

Key Cards & Chases

Major highlights include former NL MVP Ryan Howard (#6 Primetime Coach) and 2-time World Series Champion Shane Victorino (#50). Founder Jesse Cole's card is an iconic short print chase, while rookie stars like Ashby Vining and JD Urso represent the next generation of Bananaland talent.

Sourcing & Trading Advice

These cards are typically distributed at BBCL games, team stores, and regional events. If you're building a master set, raw near-mint copies range from $2 to $10, with short prints and MLB stars commanding higher premiums. Check back regularly as new cards and variations drop throughout the tour!
