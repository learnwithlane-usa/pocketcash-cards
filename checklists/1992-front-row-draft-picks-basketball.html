1992 Front Row Draft Picks Basketball
Checklist & Value Guide

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

What makes 1992 Front Row Shaq cards unique?

Front Row secured rights to photograph Shaquille O'Neal at LSU and during pre-draft workouts before he signed with the Orlando Magic, offering cards with unique college photography.

Are the prism parallels rare?

Yes, unlike standard base cards from the junk wax era, Front Row Gold Prisms were short-printed and prone to surface scratching, making PSA 9 and 10 copies legitimately scarce.
