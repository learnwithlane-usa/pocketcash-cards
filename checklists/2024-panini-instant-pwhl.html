2024 Panini Instant PWHL Inaugural Season
Checklist & Value Guide

The historic launch of the Professional Women's Hockey League (PWHL) in 2024 shattered attendance records and brought elite women's hockey to the mainstream. Panini Instant produced the league's inaugural on-demand cards, featuring living legends Marie-Philip Poulin, Taylor Heise, Sarah Nurse, and Kendall Coyne Schofield.

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Why are Panini Instant PWHL cards so scarce?

Panini Instant cards are made available online for only a few days following milestone games. The total print run matches exact customer orders before the printing plates are retired.

What is the Walter Cup?

The Walter Cup is the championship trophy of the PWHL, named after benefactors Mark and Kimbra Walter.
