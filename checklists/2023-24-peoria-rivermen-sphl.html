❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Did Peoria win the SPHL President's Cup in 2024?

Yes! The Rivermen defeated the Huntsville Havoc in a thrilling finals series to claim the 2024 President's Cup.

How long have the Peoria Rivermen been playing professional hockey?

The Rivermen franchise was founded in 1982 in the IHL, later playing in the ECHL and AHL before joining the SPHL in 2013.
