❓ Collector Insights & FAQ

Set Lore, Oddities & Print Run Notes

Q: Why do SPHL goalie and enforcer cards command premiums?

Because minor league card print runs are small (typically 500–1,500 team sets sold only at arena tables) and specific sub-niches like hockey fight collectors and mask collectors actively hunt individual cards on eBay, single standouts routinely sell for $10–$25 raw.

Q: How can you tell an authentic SPHL team issue card?

Authentic SPHL cards are standard 2.5" x 3.5" glossy cards produced by regional sports marketing agencies like Choice Marketing, featuring official team crests, player bio stats, and local arena sponsor logos on the reverse.
