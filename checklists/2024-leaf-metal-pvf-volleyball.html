❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

What is the Pro Volleyball Federation (PVF)?

The PVF is the premier North American women's professional indoor volleyball league, debuting in 2024 with teams across Atlanta, Columbus, Grand Rapids, Omaha, Orlando, San Diego, and Vegas.

Are there autographs in 2024 Leaf Metal PVF?

Yes, every hobby box guarantees three certified autographs featuring on-card signatures of top league draft picks and stars.
