1989 ProCards Minor League Baseball
Checklist & Value Guide

Produced in 1989 by ProCards, this expansive minor league release captures future Hall of Famers and generational superstars right before their MLB breakthroughs. Headlined by the legendary Ken Griffey Jr. #1 card in his San Bernardino Spirit uniform, the checklist also boasts early pre-rookie appearances of Sammy Sosa, Randy Johnson, Bernie Williams, and Curt Schilling.

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

What makes the 1989 ProCards Ken Griffey Jr. #1 so valuable?

Card #1 features Ken Griffey Jr. playing for the San Bernardino Spirit before ever taking an MLB swing with the Seattle Mariners. Because minor league distribution was regional and packaging was flimsy cellophane or baggies, PSA 9 and 10 graded copies command significant premiums.

How can you tell authentic ProCards from reprints?

Original 1989 ProCards feature distinct matte cardstock with sharp blue border lines and proper typography on the card reverse. Look out for blurry counterfeit print dots on the San Bernardino team logo.
