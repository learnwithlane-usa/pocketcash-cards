1988 Ballpark Cape Cod League Prospects
Checklist & Value Guide

Considered one of the greatest amateur baseball card releases ever produced, 1988 Ballpark Cape Cod captures future Major League MVPs and Hall of Famers before they entered professional baseball. Showcasing elite collegiate prospects playing in the premier wood-bat Cape Cod Baseball League, this 30-card set features legendary early cards of Frank Thomas, Jeff Bagwell, Jeff Kent, and Mo Vaughn.

📅 Release: Summer 1988 📦 30 Cards Complete Set in Polybag 📊 30 Cards Tracked

🔥 Key Set Highlights & Chases

  • #17
    Frank Thomas: 'The Big Hurt' playing for Orleans Cardinals. National Baseball Hall of Famer and 2x AL MVP.
  • #4
    Jeff Bagwell: Hall of Famer and NL MVP pictured as a third baseman for Chatham A's.
  • #19
    Mo Vaughn: 'The Hit Dog' Wareham Gatemen slugger and 1995 AL MVP.
  • #18
    Chuck Knoblauch: Wareham Gatemen leadoff sparkplug and 1991 AL Rookie of the Year.

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Why are 1988 Cape Cod League cards so collectible?

The Cape Cod Baseball League is the gold standard of collegiate summer baseball. The 1988 roster featured multiple future MLB MVPs and Hall of Famers before they signed pro contracts.
