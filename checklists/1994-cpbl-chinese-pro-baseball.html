❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

What is CPBL baseball?

The Chinese Professional Baseball League (CPBL) is the top tier professional baseball league in Taiwan, founded in 1989.

Why are vintage 1990s CPBL cards rare?

Most early CPBL cards were distributed exclusively in Taiwan and have low survival rates, making pack-fresh copies highly sought after by Asian baseball collectors.
