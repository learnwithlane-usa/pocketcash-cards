2023 Choice Lansing Lugnuts
Checklist & Value Guide

Released by Choice SportsCards for the 2023 High-A Midwest League campaign, this 37-card Lansing Lugnuts team set features two consecutive Oakland Athletics 1st-round draft picks: catcher Daniel Susac (#01, 19th overall in 2022) and shortstop Max Muncy (#20, 25th overall in 2021). Also included are MLB pitchers Brady Basso (#05), Grant Holman (#14), Jack Perkins (#22), and high-ceiling draftees Clark Elliott (#11) and Colby Thomas.
