🏷️ Historic First Major Bowling Card Set

1990 Collect-A-Card Kingpins PBA Bowling
Checklist & Value Guide

Released in 1990 by Collect-A-Card, Kingpins stands as the first comprehensive, mainstream trading card set dedicated to the stars and Hall of Fame legends of the Professional Bowlers Association (PBA). Featuring 100 base cards, this landmark release houses the official rookie cards for Earl Anthony (#1), Norm Duke (#13), Pete Weber (#23), and Walter Ray Williams Jr. (#51).

📅 Release: 1990 📦 36 Packs Per Box / 10 Cards Per Pack 📊 100 Cards Tracked
🔍 Browse All 1990 Collect-A-Card Kingpins PBA Bowling Cards & Wax on eBay

🔥 Key Set Highlights & Chases

  • #1
    Earl Anthony RC: The 43-time PBA Tour champion and first bowler to earn $1,000,000 in career earnings. The foundational grail card of modern bowling collecting.
  • #23
    Pete Weber RC: 'PDW' — 37 PBA Tour titles, 10 majors, and one of the most electric, polarizing cultural icons in bowling history.
  • #13
    Norm Duke RC: 38 PBA Tour titles, 7 majors, and one of only three bowlers in history to achieve the Grand Slam.
  • #51
    Walter Ray Williams Jr. RC: All-time record 47 PBA Tour titles and 7x PBA Player of the Year.
