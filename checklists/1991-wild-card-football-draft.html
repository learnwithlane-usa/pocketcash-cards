🏷️ Pioneer of Parallel Multiplier Stripes

1991 Wild Card Football Draft
Checklist & Value Guide

Wild Card changed the trading card hobby forever in 1991 by introducing redemption stripes: 5-Stripe, 10-Stripe, 20-Stripe, 50-Stripe, 100-Stripe, and the mythical 1000-Stripe. Featuring collegiate draft stars in their NCAA uniforms, the set is anchored by Hall of Famer Brett Favre (#119 Southern Miss) and college phenom Raghib 'Rocket' Ismail.

📅 Release: 1991 📦 36 Packs Per Box / 15 Cards Per Pack 📊 15 Cards Tracked

🔥 Key Set Highlights & Chases

  • #119
    Brett Favre RC (Southern Miss): The definitive pre-draft card of 3-time NFL MVP Brett Favre. High-stripe versions are four-figure grails.
  • #1
    Raghib 'Rocket' Ismail (Notre Dame): The most hyped player in college football who bypassed the NFL to sign with the CFL Toronto Argonauts.
  • #110
    Aeneas Williams RC (Southern): Hall of Fame cornerback rookie card with scarce stripe parallels.
  • 1000-Stripe
    1000-Stripe Parallels: Extremely low pack-odds multiplier cards redeemable for 1,000 standard cards. Holy grail oddball collectibles.

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

What do the stripes mean on 1991 Wild Card cards?

Wild Card allowed collectors to mail in striped cards in exchange for the printed number of regular base cards (e.g. a 20-Stripe could be mailed in for 20 base cards). Unredeemed high-stripe cards, especially 100-Stripe and 1000-Stripe, are heavily sought after today.

How valuable is the Brett Favre 1000-Stripe?

The 1991 Wild Card 1000-Stripe Brett Favre is considered one of the holy grails of early 1990s football cards, with confirmed private sales reaching into four figures.
