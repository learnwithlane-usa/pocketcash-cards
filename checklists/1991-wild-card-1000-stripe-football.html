🏷️ The Original 90s Multiplier Stripe Parallel

1991 Wild Card 1000 Stripe Football Multipliers
Checklist & Value Guide

Released in 1991 by AAA Sports, Wild Card revolutionized the sports card hobby by introducing the first tiered redemption parallel system: cards with 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, and the legendary 1,000 Stripe multiplier. Headlined by Brett Favre's iconic Southern Miss rookie card.

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

How did the Wild Card stripe redemption system work?

Collectors could mail a 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, or 1,000 Stripe card to Wild Card and receive that exact number of standard base cards in return, or keep the rare multiplier foil card as a permanent collectible.

What is a 1991 Wild Card Brett Favre 1,000 Stripe worth?

Authenticated copies of Brett Favre's 1,000 Stripe routinely fetch $3,000 to $10,000+ at major sports card auctions due to extreme scarcity.
