Produced in 1995 by Wheels / High Gear, this vintage rodeo set is one of the definitive trading card releases for professional rodeo and PRCA world champions. Highlighting fearless bull riders, steer wrestlers, saddle bronc champions, and legendary bullfighters, singles command steady demand from Western sports collectors.
