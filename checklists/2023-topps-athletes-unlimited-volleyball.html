❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

How does Athletes Unlimited Volleyball scoring work?

Players earn individual points for match wins, set wins, MVPs of the match, and positive statistics (kills, digs, aces, blocks) while losing points for errors.

Are there certified autographs in 2023 Topps AU Volleyball?

Yes! Select box sets feature authentic on-card and sticker certified autographs of Jordan Larson, Leah Edmond, and Bethania De La Cruz.
