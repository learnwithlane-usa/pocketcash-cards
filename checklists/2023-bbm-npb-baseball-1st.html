2023 BBM 1st Version NPB Japanese Baseball
Checklist & Value Guide

❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

What is the difference between BBM 1st Version and 2nd Version?

BBM 1st Version focuses on the main active roster and top veterans at the beginning of the NPB season, while 2nd Version includes mid-season call-ups, traded players, and update subsets.

How do BBM print runs compare to American cards like Topps?

BBM print runs are significantly lower than American flagship products, making clean ungraded copies harder to locate outside Japan.
