❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Are Leaf soccer cards officially licensed by FIFA or UEFA?

Leaf soccer cards are unlicenced by FIFA, meaning club crests and team names are omitted. However, Leaf holds direct autograph contracts with the athletes themselves, making their certified autographs 100% authentic and highly prized.

Why do collectors buy Leaf soccer instead of Panini Prizm?

Leaf Metal offers guaranteed on-card autographs and significantly lower entry prices compared to Panini Prizm or Topps Chrome hobby boxes.
