❓ Collector Insights & FAQ

Set Lore, Oddities & Print Run Notes

Q: Are the Matt Guy and MLW Wiffle Ball cards official rookie cards?

Yes! The 2023 Topps Allen & Ginter release marks the first officially licensed, nationally distributed trading cards and certified autographs for Matt Guy (ACL Cornhole) and Kyle Schultz/Tommy Coughlin (MLW Wiffle Ball). They are considered true rookie cards by alternative sports collectors.

Q: What parallels exist for The Ocho cards in 2023 Allen & Ginter?

Cards exist in standard full-size base, Silver Portrait, Gold Border, Glossy 1/1, and the classic 1.4" x 2.8" Mini cards (A&G Back, Black Border, No Number /50, Brooklyn Back /25, Wood 1/1, and Framed Mini Autographs).
