❓ Collector Insights & FAQ

Set Lore, Oddities & Print Run Notes

Q: What is the American Association of Professional Baseball (AAPB)?

The American Association is an official MLB Partner League founded in 2005. It features 12 clubs across the American Midwest, Texas, and Canada, known for launching dozens of players directly back into Major League Baseball organizations each summer.

Q: Who prints authentic AAPB team sets?

The official cards are manufactured by Choice SportsCards (Choice Marketing), packaged in shrink-wrapped team sets, and sold through team pro shops at ballparks.
