❓ Collector Insights & FAQ

Set Lore, Oddities & Print Run Notes

Q: Why are CPBL cheerleader cards so valuable?

Taiwanese cheerleader culture is an integral part of CPBL fandom. Cheerleaders like Lin Xiang, Yuri, and Qun Qun have millions of mainstream followers across Taiwan, Japan, and the United States. Limited holo foil cards, autographed polaroids, and uniform patch cards routinely sell for $50 to $300+ on eBay.

Q: How can US collectors obtain CPBL cards?

While CPBL boxes are sold at 7-Eleven stores and team stadiums in Taiwan, Pocket Cash Cards and dedicated eBay sellers import sealed boxes and singles with global eBay International Shipping.
