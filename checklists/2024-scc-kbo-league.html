❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Are KBO cards officially licensed?

Yes, SCC is the officially licensed card manufacturer of the Korea Baseball Organization (KBO), featuring authentic team logos, jerseys, and player likenesses.

Why are KBO cards gaining popularity in the US?

With stars like Jung Hoo Lee and Ha-Seong Kim making huge impacts in MLB, American collectors actively target KBO cards to find future international posting stars early.
