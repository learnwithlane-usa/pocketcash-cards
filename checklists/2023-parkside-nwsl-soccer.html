2023 Parkside NWSL Women's Soccer
Checklist & Value Guide

Parkside Collectibles established the gold standard for women's soccer cards with their 2023 NWSL release. Capturing an explosive era in North American soccer, the set features top chases for Trinity Rodman, league MVP Sophia Smith, teen sensation Alyssa Thompson, and farewell cards for legend Megan Rapinoe.
