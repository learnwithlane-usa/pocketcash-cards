2023 Leaf Pickleball Premier Edition
Card Checklist & Price Guide

Released on October 13, 2023, Leaf Pickleball Premier Edition marks the first-ever standalone trading card release in the history of pickleball. Featuring 40 total base cards, every single card represents a true 1st Standalone Rookie Card for the sport's biggest icons—headlined by teenage sensation Anna Leigh Waters (#1), men's GOAT Ben Johns (#10), tour superstar Tyson McGuffin (#39), and Super Bowl champion team owner Drew Brees (#3).

📅 Release: Oct 13, 2023 📦 5 Cards Per Box (1 Auto + 1 Parallel) 📊 40 Base Cards (#1–#41, #31 unissued) ⭐ 1/1 Black Holo Grails

How to Collect & Price 2023 Leaf Pickleball Cards

Because pickleball is the fastest growing sport across North America, player recognition has exploded while printed trading card supply remains remarkably small. Unlike modern baseball or basketball releases printed in six-figure unit runs, 2023 Leaf Pickleball was produced in strictly limited quantities.

01. The ALW Effect

Anna Leigh Waters Scarcity

Anna Leigh Waters is the single biggest draw in the sport. Raw base cards routinely trade for $15–$35, while numbered autos and PSA 10 slabs reach $150 to $400+.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most valuable card in 2023 Leaf Pickleball?

The #1 Anna Leigh Waters 1/1 Black Holo Auto and Gold Holo /5 Autographs are the most valuable cards in the release, followed by Ben Johns (#10) and Drew Brees (#3) low-numbered autograph parallels.

Why is card #31 missing from the checklist?

Card #31 was intentionally omitted / unissued by Leaf during final production scheduling. The base set consists of exactly 40 cards numbered 1 through 41 (excluding #31).

Why are Drew Brees and Hannah Johns included?

Drew Brees is a high-profile team owner in Major League Pickleball (LA Mad Drops). Hannah Johns is the lead broadcast sideline reporter for PPA tour telecasts and sister to Ben and Collin Johns.

Are these considered true Rookie Cards (RC)?

Yes. Because 2023 Leaf Pickleball Premier Edition was the first standalone, packaged trading card product ever produced for pickleball, all player cards in this set serve as their official 1st Standalone Rookie Cards.
