❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

Why is Onosato's 2024 rookie card so valuable?

Onosato reached the rank of Ozeki in just 9 professional tournaments (basho), breaking all modern historical records and creating unprecedented international bidding demand.

What is a Tegata card?

A Tegata is an actual inked handprint stamped by the wrestler, accompanied by their handwritten calligraphy and signature, serving as the highest grail hit in Japanese sumo collecting.
