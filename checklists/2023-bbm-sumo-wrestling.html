❓ Collector Insights & FAQ

Set Lore, Oddities & Print Run Notes

Q: What is a Sumo Tegata card?

A Tegata is an ancient Japanese tradition where a high-ranking Sekitori or Yokozuna presses their palm in black or red ink onto paper, accompanied by their ring name (shikona) in sumi calligraphy and official stable seal. BBM inserts authentic mini-tegata cards into select hobby boxes.

Q: How often does BBM release sumo cards?

BBM typically releases two flagship sumo sets per year (often timed with the New Year Tokyo Hatsu Basho and autumn tournaments), alongside occasional commemorative tribute releases for retired Yokozuna.
