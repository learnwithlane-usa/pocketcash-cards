❓ Collector Insights & FAQs

What makes Cape Cod League cards so unique?

Cape Cod League cards are produced in extremely small runs and are sold almost exclusively at local Cape Cod high school ballparks during June and July games. They capture collegiate stars using wood bats before any professional card company can sign them.

How many CCBL alumni make MLB rosters?

More than 1 in 6 active Major League Baseball players played in the Cape Cod Baseball League, making CCBL cards top targets for early prospect hunters.
