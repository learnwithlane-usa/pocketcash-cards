How does the Athletes Unlimited format work?

Athletes Unlimited is a player-driven league without traditional team owners. Players score individual points each play and re-draft teams every single week, crowning an individual champion.

Yes! Each hobby box contains an average of two certified autographs or premium memorabilia relic cards.
